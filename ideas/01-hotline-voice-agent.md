## Problem Statement

AI agents are getting wallets. Every agent-payments demo shows the agent spending. Almost none show the human being asked first, in a way that reaches them wherever they are. Screens need an app open. Push notifications get swiped. A phone call interrupts.

Hotline is an onchain agent on Arc testnet you reach by dialing a phone number. You talk, it acts within limits you set. When something happens onchain that crosses a limit, it calls *you*, explains, and waits for a spoken yes. The phone call is the guardrail.

Target: UBC 2026 Hackathon, Agentic Payments & AI track, Best use of Arc, ElevenLabs + QuickNode bounties.

## What Makes This Cool

- **The phone rings on stage.** Mid-demo, a testnet USDC transfer lands in the agent's wallet. The presenter's phone rings. The agent explains what arrived and asks permission to act. The room hears the whole thing.
- **Guardrails you can hear.** "That's above your 50 USDC limit, say yes and your PIN to confirm." Policy tiers aren't a config page, they're dialogue.
- **No app.** Any phone. Zero install.
- **The agent pays its own way (stretch).** It buys a price quote from a 402-gated API for a fraction of a USDC, and the ledger shows the micropayment next to the human-approved transfers.

## Premises (agreed)

1. The phone call is the guardrail. All prior voice-crypto projects found (Cymatic, Call DeFAI Engine, Baseline, VoiceVault, several ETHGlobal entries) are inbound-only. Outbound approval calls are the differentiator and map to the track's "human approval for large transactions."
2. Arc **testnet**, faucet USDC, zero real funds. Our policy engine is the enforcement layer.
3. Solo TypeScript, zero custom contracts, **one wallet layer** (decided by a pre-hack spike, see Open Q1).
4. Trigger pipeline: onchain deposit → webhook or RPC watcher → decision loop → ElevenLabs outbound call. QuickNode Streams is the primary trigger for the bounty; an RPC event poller is the fallback that keeps the phone ringing.
5. Core scope is deliberately small: inbound (status, send, pause), outbound approval on deposit, policy tiers, ledger, dashboard. x402 and swap/bridge are stretch, in that order.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Minimal core, "Ring and Act"
ElevenLabs agent on a Twilio number; Node backend on Arc testnet; inbound status / send / pause; outbound approval call on deposit; policy engine with caps, allowlist, approval threshold, PIN; ledger; dashboard. Action kinds: `send` only.
Effort M. Risk Low-Med. **This is also the fallback: if any cut line is missed, the submission is exactly this.**

### Approach B: Policy engine is the product (CHOSEN)
Approach A plus, as stretch in order: (1) x402-shaped `pay_api` flow, (2) swap or CCTP bridge. Circle wallet SDK if the spike says it works on Arc testnet.
Effort L. Risk Med. Mitigated by the cut lines and by A being a complete submission on its own.

### Approach C: Outbound only
A watchdog you talk to once, then it only ever calls you. Sharpest story, fewest parts, but loses the inbound conversation the ElevenLabs judge wants.

## Recommended Approach

Approach B, built as A first, then stretch items strictly in order, with the cut lines below.

### Architecture

```
  Owner phone <── Twilio (number imported into ElevenLabs, native integration, both directions) ──> ElevenLabs Agent
       ^                                                                                              │ server tools (HTTPS, X-Hotline-Secret)
       │ POST /v1/convai/twilio/outbound-call (from backend)                                          │ conversation-initiation webhook (inbound)
       │                                                                                              │ post-call webhook (HMAC) → call outcome + transcript
       └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  Backend (Node 22 / TypeScript / Hono)
                                                                                                   ├── Policy engine → AUTO | CALL | BLOCK
                                                                                                   ├── Ledger (node:sqlite, Node >= 22.13 pinned, on a persistent volume)
                                                                                                   ├── Wallet layer (ONE of: Circle Dev-Controlled Wallets on ARC-TESTNET, or viem + env key)
                                                                                                   ├── Chain reads via viem (Arc testnet RPC from QuickNode)
                                                                                                   ├── Deposit trigger: QuickNode Streams webhook (primary) + viem watchContractEvent poller (fallback); dedup makes the loser a no-op
                                                                                                   ├── Outbound call queue (one call, one pending action, at a time per owner)
                                                                                                   ├── GET /api/* (read) + POST /api/* (unpause, simulate; DASHBOARD_TOKEN required) → Dashboard (single page, 2 s poll)
                                                                                                   └── x402-shaped client + gated /quote endpoint (stretch 1)
```

**Identity and auth**
- Owner phone numbers live in config (`OWNER_NUMBERS`, E.164). ElevenLabs passes `system__caller_id` and `system__called_number` as server-tool parameters (both must be configured explicitly in every tool definition; they are not automatic). Backend normalizes to E.164 (strip spaces, dashes, parens; ensure leading `+`) before comparing.
- Owner gate, inbound: `caller_id ∈ OWNER_NUMBERS`. Owner gate, outbound: the `conversation_id` is in the backend's outbound map (the backend placed the call, so it knows who it dialled), or `called_number ∈ OWNER_NUMBERS`. Any tool call failing both is rejected and the agent says it can't help.
- A 4-digit spoken PIN (`OWNER_PIN`) is required before any CALL-tier confirmation. The agent prompt instructs it to pass digits only; the backend strips non-digits (STT yields "forty-seven twenty-one" as often as "4 7 2 1"). Wrong PIN → one retry, then cancel with reason `bad_pin`. **Demo-day PIN is rotated after the event**, because it is spoken in a room and stored in ElevenLabs transcripts. Digit runs of 4+ are stripped from `transcript_snippet` before storage.
- Rules cannot be changed by voice at all.
- Known limitations, stated in README: caller ID can be spoofed; PIN mitigates, does not eliminate; PIN is audible. Production would use Twilio Verify or a callback.
- Every ledger row stores `caller_id` and `conversation_id`.
- Server tools authenticate with `X-Hotline-Secret`, a per-deploy env var. Deposit webhook verifies QuickNode's HMAC security token. ElevenLabs post-call webhook verifies its HMAC signature. Dashboard POSTs require `DASHBOARD_TOKEN`; "simulate deposit" is disabled unless `NODE_ENV !== production`.

**Call lifecycle plumbing**
- **Inbound:** ElevenLabs conversation-initiation webhook fires at call start; backend returns dynamic variables including `pending_summary` (any CALL-tier action awaiting a decision) and `owner_name`. This is deterministic, unlike hoping the LLM calls a tool first. `get_status` remains as the belt.
- **Outbound:** backend places the call and records `conversation_id → {owner, action_code}`.
- **Post-call webhook** (one endpoint, three jobs): marks the call ended, stores the transcript and the "yes" snippet, and, for outbound calls that ended with no decision, triggers the no-answer logic. Fallback if the webhook is unreliable: poll `GET /v1/convai/conversations/{id}` every 10 s during the 10-minute expiry window.

**ElevenLabs server tools** (each returns within ~15 s; nothing waits on chain receipts):
- `get_status()` → USDC balance, gas reserve, paused flag, last 3 ledger rows, `pending_actions[]`.
- `propose_action(kind, to_label, amount_usdc)` → runs policy engine. `to_label` **must** be an allowlist label; the agent reads labels from `get_rules`; raw addresses are refused. Returns `{action_code, decision, reason, summary, status}`. AUTO: submits and returns `status: submitted`; agent says "sending now." CALL: "needs confirmation." BLOCK: the rule name.
- `confirm_action(approved: bool, pin?: string)` → acts on the action most recently proposed or presented **in this conversation** (server-side map keyed by `conversation_id`; the LLM never repeats an ID). An inbound-proposed action is always bound to its own conversation and never to a queued outbound sweep, so a stale pending sweep can never be executed by a "yes" meant for a different action. Idempotent: a second call returns the original result without re-executing; a mismatched `approved` returns an error. When the agent cancels (`approved: false`), the backend sets `reason` from context (`declined`, `unclear`, `superseded`, `bad_pin`); there is no `reason` parameter.
- `get_action_status()` → lifecycle status of the most recent action on this conversation plus first 6 hex chars of the tx hash. Agent speaks "done, hash starts 0x3f2a… full link is on the dashboard."
- `get_rules()` → caps, allowlist labels, threshold, sweep config. Read-only by voice.
- `pause()` → `paused=true`. Unpause is dashboard-only (token-protected POST).

**Action codes:** short 6-char codes (e.g. `H-7K2Q`) for ledger and dashboard. Voice never needs them.

**Policy engine** (`decide(action) → AUTO | CALL | BLOCK`, in order):
1. `paused` → BLOCK `paused`
2. `to_label` not in allowlist → BLOCK `not_allowlisted` (allowlist is label → address, config/dashboard only; includes `savings` and, for stretch 1, `quote_api`)
3. `amount > PER_TX_CAP` → BLOCK `per_tx_cap`
4. `spent_today + amount > DAILY_CAP` → BLOCK `daily_cap`. `spent_today` sums rows with status `submitted` or `complete` since `DAILY_RESET_UTC` (default 00:00 UTC).
5. `balance - amount < GAS_RESERVE_USDC` → BLOCK `gas_reserve` (USDC is gas on Arc)
6. `amount > APPROVAL_THRESHOLD` or `source == stream` → CALL
7. else → AUTO

"Amount" is the USDC leaving the wallet. `pay_api` amounts accrue to the daily cap. Swap/bridge, if built, use the USDC input amount. A stream-sourced sweep that BLOCKs on rules 1–5 writes an `observed` row with the blocking rule and does **not** call; it is visible on the dashboard. This makes the demo config below load-bearing.

**Ledger row:** `code, ts, source (voice|stream|agent), caller_id, conversation_id, kind, to_label, to_addr, amount, decision (AUTO|CALL|BLOCK|observed), rule, status, error, tx_hash, log_index, transcript_snippet`.
`status` is lifecycle only: `pending → approved | cancelled | expired | expired_no_answer`; `approved → submitted → complete | failed`; AUTO actions start at `submitted`. Unique constraint on `(tx_hash, log_index)` for deposit-sourced rows. On boot: re-poll every `submitted` row; expire every `pending` row older than 10 minutes.

**Confirmation semantics** (in the agent's system prompt and tested):
- Only an unambiguous affirmative plus a PIN calls `confirm_action(true, pin)`.
- "Maybe", "wait", a question → agent answers or re-asks, max 2, then `confirm_action(false)` (backend reason `unclear`).
- Counter-proposal ("send 80 instead") → `propose_action` with the new amount; the old pending action is cancelled server-side (`superseded`).
- The spoken "yes" snippet (digits stripped) is stored in `transcript_snippet`.

**Sweep rule** (the only standing rule in the build; config/dashboard only):
`SWEEP_THRESHOLD_USDC` and `SAVINGS_LABEL`. On deposit, compute `available = balance - sum(outflows in pending|approved|submitted)`. If `available > SWEEP_THRESHOLD_USDC`, propose `send(SAVINGS_LABEL, available - SWEEP_THRESHOLD_USDC - GAS_RESERVE_USDC)`; otherwise write an `observed` row and do not call. The amount is **recomputed at dial time and again at confirm time** with the same formula, so a sweep never double-counts funds already committed. If a sweep is already pending when a new deposit arrives, the pending sweep is marked `superseded` and one new sweep is proposed for the recomputed excess; if the outbound call for the old sweep is already live, the new deposit instead writes `observed: sweep_pending` and the live call's confirm-time recomputation picks up the larger excess. A general `standing_rules` YAML schema goes in the README under "what's next," not in the build.

**Deposit trigger and outbound flow (the climax):**
1. Primary: QuickNode Stream, Arc testnet, `logs` dataset, filter `address == USDC_TESTNET && topic0 == keccak(Transfer(address,address,uint256)) && topic2 == pad32(agent_wallet)`, webhook destination. Fallback: viem `watchContractEvent` on the same filter via RPC, built **first** on Mon. Both run in the demo; `(tx_hash, log_index)` dedup makes whichever arrives second a no-op, and the ledger records which source won.
2. Webhook handler: verify HMAC → iterate every log in the batch → `INSERT OR IGNORE` → only newly inserted rows proceed.
3. Sweep rule → action → policy engine → CALL (or `observed`).
4. Outbound queue: **one active outbound call per owner, and one pending stream-sourced action per owner** (a second deposit supersedes rather than queues, per the sweep rule above). If an inbound call is live, hold; the action appears in `pending_summary` on the next inbound call and in `get_status`; if the live call ends without a decision, dial out. Inbound-proposed CALL actions are independent of the outbound queue: an owner can call in and propose `send(alice, 80)` while a sweep is pending, and each is bound to its own conversation.
5. Outbound call via ElevenLabs with `dynamic_variables = {action_code, summary, amount, to_label, rule_name, balance}`; the system prompt references these.
6. No answer or machine: retry once after 90 s, then `expired_no_answer`. Any `pending` action not decided within 10 minutes → `expired`. Expired actions stay on the dashboard with "call in to re-propose." Whether the ElevenLabs outbound endpoint exposes Twilio answering-machine detection is unverified (Open Q6); fallback is a prompt instruction ("if no human responds within 10 seconds, say goodbye and end the call") plus the expiry.
7. Execution: submit, return immediately, poll receipt in the background (Circle: poll for `COMPLETE`; viem: `waitForTransactionReceipt`). On revert/timeout/insufficient balance → `failed` with the error; if still on the call, the agent says "that didn't go through, it's on the dashboard, nothing was sent."

**Dashboard:** one page, 2 s poll. Balance, rules, sweep config, ledger table with lifecycle status and explorer links, transcript (fetched lazily by `conversation_id`), unpause button, "simulate deposit" button (non-production only; disclosed in README if it is ever used in the demo).

**x402-shaped stretch (honest scope):** backend hosts `GET /quote?pair=ETH-USD` returning 402 with `{amount, pay_to}` where `pay_to` is the `quote_api` allowlist address. The agent's `get_quote` tool calls `propose_action(pay_api, quote_api, amount)` (AUTO under threshold), then retries with `X-Payment-Tx`; the server verifies the tx on chain and records it in `used_payment_tx` so a hash buys exactly one quote. Whether Arc testnet USDC supports EIP-3009 (spec x402 "exact") is unverified; label as "x402-shaped, not spec-compliant" unless the spike proves otherwise. Ledger `kind: pay_api, source: agent`.
